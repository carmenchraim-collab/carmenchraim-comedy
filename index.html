About

Born and raised in Lebanon, Carmen started doing stand-up in Berlin back when the city had exactly one open mic. She helped build the scene from there, producing shows like Comedy Auf Deuglish, Teepeeland Stands Up and Stand Up Saturdays, and co-hosting Monday Night Mics, one of Berlin's longest-running weekly shows.

She's performed on Comedy Central Germany, played the house team at Comedy Café Berlin, toured her one-hour solo show, and founded SUFTL, Hamburg's feminist comedy stage. Today she performs with the Queen Bees Comedy collective.

On stage she works the room hard. Every look at the audience pulls another thread, until the whole crowd is caught in one very tight web.
