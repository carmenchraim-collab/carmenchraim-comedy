About

Born and raised in Lebanon, Carmen has been doing stand-up in Berlin since 2013, back in the days when there were two open mics in the whole city. Yes, she's that much of an OG. She brings high energy, quick stories and a love for sarcasm to every stage, and she's known for being petite but very, very loud.

Her material comes from life as a Lebanese woman in Germany: work, integration, the news, and everything that gets lost in translation along the way. She loves talking to the crowd, so no two shows are ever the same. The front row has been warned.

Over more than ten years, she's performed in clubs, in a circus tent, in a conference room and in a cellar with no fire escape. She's produced shows, hosted shows, played solo shows in Berlin and across Europe, and opened for famous comedians who no longer remember her. Today she's part of the Queen Bees Comedy collective.
